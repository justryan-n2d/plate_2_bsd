# Phase 1 Site Base Files

This folder contains preliminary outputs for the lot-and-site-base phase of CE 393 Plate No. 2.

- phase1_site_base.svg: editable vector sketch. It shows both the original P5-1 value and the adjusted closing course.
- phase1_traverse_coordinates.csv: course bearings, distances, calculated latitude and departure, local endpoint coordinates, and a separate closure-gap row.
- phase1_site_analysis.md: known site conditions and outstanding questions.

## Important status

The original five listed courses produce a linear misclosure of about 4.0120 m. Following the instructor's direction to close the traverse, courses 1-2 through 4-5 are kept as given and only P5-1 is adjusted. The original P5-1 value (N 08°10′ W, 16.35 m) remains in the CSV for reference; the adjusted closing course is N 06°02′11″ E, 15.7954 m (about 15.80 m). The closed plot has a perimeter of 64.1454 m and a calculated area of 265.26 m², compared with the approximate 300.00 m² in the brief. The difference is documented, not hidden.

Use 5.0 m as the road width and 8.00 m as the total right-of-way. The remaining 3.0 m may be used for shoulders, drainage, or other road-related spaces depending on the plan; the assignment does not specify the allocation. These files are preliminary academic site-base materials, not cadastral survey or construction documents.
