# Phase 1 Site Base Files

This folder contains preliminary lot-and-site-base outputs for CE 393 Plate No. 2. Phase 1 covers the lot base and source-based site analysis only. No house layout has been started.

## Files

- `phase1_site_base.svg`: editable vector sketch showing the original P5-1 value and the adjusted closing course, local north arrow, adjoining lots, road context, area comparison, and notes.
- `phase1_traverse_coordinates.csv`: course bearings, distances, calculated latitude and departure, local coordinates, and a separate diagnostic closure-gap row.
- `phase1_site_analysis.md`: site analysis based on the assignment, including access, solar-orientation limits, existing conditions, regional climate context, and site-verification items.
- `phase1_site_analysis_matrix.csv`: checklist matrix linking each required site-analysis topic to its current finding and follow-up action.

## Traverse and area status

The original five listed courses produce a linear misclosure of about 4.0120 m. Following the instructor's direction to close the traverse, courses 1-2 through 4-5 are kept as given and only P5-1 is adjusted. The original P5-1 value (N 08°10' W, 16.35 m) remains in the records; the adjusted closing course is N 06°02'11" E, 15.7954 m (about 15.80 m).

The adjusted closed plot has a perimeter of 64.1454 m and calculated area of 265.26 m², compared with the assignment's approximate 300.00 m². The 34.74 m² difference is documented and still needs checking against the source survey plan. Do not force the closed plot to match the approximate area.

## Road width and right-of-way

Use 5.0 m as the existing road width and 8.00 m as the total right-of-way, because the assignment provides both as separate values. The remaining 3.0 m may accommodate shoulders, drainage, or other road-related spaces depending on the actual plan. The assignment does not specify the allocation, and these files do not assume exact road-edge or ROW offsets.

## Site analysis status

The written site analysis covers lot orientation, north direction, road frontage, preliminary solar-orientation limits, vehicular/pedestrian/service access, existing-condition information, nearby regional climate data, and the geohazard-verification need. Exact access points, parcel-specific solar/shade exposure, actual drainage/topography, and site-specific hazard results remain unverified because reliable parcel coordinates, site photos, and field observations were not provided.

Regional weather context is referenced to PAGASA's Tacloban City 1991-2020 climatological normals. HazardHunterPH is listed as a verification tool, not as a completed property-specific hazard assessment.

These are preliminary academic site-base materials, not cadastral survey or construction documents.
