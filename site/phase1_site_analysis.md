## 5. Regional climate note for design awareness

The [PAGASA Tacloban City climatological normals for 1991–2020](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/cad/CLIMATOLOGICAL%20NORMALS%20%281991-2020%29/TACLOBAN.pdf) report **2,840.6 mm annual rainfall**, **182 rainy days**, **28.1°C mean temperature**, and a **northwest prevailing wind averaging 2 m/s** for the Tacloban station. Palo and the subject lot are not the station itself, so these figures are only a nearby regional reference and must not be described as measurements taken on the lot.

For the academic design, consider rain runoff, openings for natural ventilation, shading, and covered pedestrian/entry access when arranging the building. No site-specific rainfall, shadow, wind, flood, or geotechnical measurement is assumed.
