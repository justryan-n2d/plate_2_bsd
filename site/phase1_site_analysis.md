## 2. Orientation, traverse adjustment, and area

The plotted lot uses a local coordinate origin of **P1 = (0, 0)**, with positive eastings to the east and positive northings to the north. These are local plotting coordinates, not surveyed geographic coordinates.

The original five stated courses produce a latitude sum of **+0.4764 m**, a departure sum of **−3.9836 m**, and a linear misclosure of about **4.0120 m**. Following the instructor's direction to close the traverse, courses **1-2, 2-3, 3-4, and 4-5 are held unchanged** and only P5-1 is adjusted to connect P5 back to P1.

| P5-1 item | Bearing | Distance |
|---|---|---:|
| Original value given in the assignment | N 08°10′ W | 16.35 m |
| Adjusted closing course used in the plot | N 06°02′11″ E | 15.7954 m (about 15.80 m) |

To close from P5, the required latitude component is **+15.707836 m** and the required departure component is **+1.661019 m**. The adjusted course is calculated from those components. The original value is kept in the CSV for transparency; it is not the line used to close the revised plot.

Using this adjusted closing course, the calculated perimeter is **64.1454 m** and the polygon area is **265.26 m²**. The assignment's stated area is approximately **300.00 m²**, so the area from this closed plot is about **34.74 m² lower** than that approximate figure. Both numbers are documented rather than forcing the polygon to match the approximate area.
