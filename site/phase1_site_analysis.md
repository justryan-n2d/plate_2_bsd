## 5. Regional climate note for preliminary design awareness

The official [PAGASA Tacloban City climatological normals for 1991-2020](https://pubfiles.pagasa.dost.gov.ph/pagasaweb/files/cad/CLIMATOLOGICAL%20NORMALS%20%281991-2020%29/TACLOBAN.pdf) report **2,840.6 mm annual rainfall**, **182 rainy days**, **28.1°C mean temperature**, and **northwest prevailing wind at an average 2 m/s** for the Tacloban station. Palo and the subject parcel are not the station itself, so these values are a nearby regional reference only and must not be described as measurements taken on the lot.

Practical items to inspect during a site visit:
- Signs of ponding, overland runoff, roadside drainage, and low points after rain.
- Actual surface levels at both road frontages and at the lot corners.
- Roof/runoff discharge constraints and whether access paths would obstruct drainage, once a site-development plan is prepared.
- Existing shade, nearby walls/buildings, and openings or trees that may affect sunlight and natural ventilation.
- Whether site-specific wind and sun exposure differs because of neighbouring buildings, trees, and obstructions.

For a location-based hazard check, [HazardHunterPH](https://hazardhunter.georisk.gov.ph/) provides an indicative assessment using government-agency hazard information. The exact lot has not been pinpointed, so this report does not assign a flood, storm-surge, earthquake, liquefaction, landslide, or severe-wind rating to it. HazardHunterPH also advises users to verify results and consult the responsible agencies for official assessments.
