# Phase 3 Space Planning - Revision 03

This folder holds the area-budget-coordinated room programme for CE 393 Plate No. 2.

## Files

- [Room requirement table](space_requirement_table.csv): assignment spaces plus the additional spaces shown in the student's reference.
- [Adjacency matrix](adjacency_matrix.csv): reasons for direct, near, buffered and conditional relationships.
- [Adjacency diagram](adjacency_diagram.svg): room-relationship overview.
- [Ground-floor zoning](ground_floor_zoning.svg): first-floor room arrangement, TV in living room and unobstructed foyer route.
- [Second-floor zoning](second_floor_zoning.svg): second-floor room arrangement with gym access from the shared hall and one karaoke-room door.
- [Phase 3 planning notes](space_planning.md): rationale, proposed targets and hold points.
- [Area-budget calculation CSV](../calculations/phase3_area_budget.csv): programme sums, gross-area proxies and provisional site coverage comparisons.
- [Space-planning validator](../scripts/validate_space_planning.py): checks schedule arithmetic, area budget, SVG contents and layout guardrails.

## Important limits

All new sizes are proposed area-budget targets, not dimensions measured from the image and not code minimums. The inherited 15% walls/structure allowance is rough only. The 2.00 m offset polygon and 90% corner-lot coverage scenario do not replace parcel-specific Palo zoning, road ROW and setback confirmation. The final footprint and GFA require measured plans.
